# Vera – Business Plan

## Executive Summary

Vera is a turnkey social media architecture engineered for the 2026 regulatory
landscape.  It solves the two greatest liabilities facing every social platform
today: **Minor Safety** and **Data Privacy** – while generating a sustainable,
ad-free revenue stream.

## Problem

1. **Minors on adult platforms** – regulators (Canada's Bill C-63, UK's Online
   Safety Act, EU's DSA) are imposing heavy fines on platforms that fail to
   prevent minors from accessing adult content.
2. **Data harvesting liability** – PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25, and GDPR create
   massive legal exposure for platforms that store personal information.
3. **Bot farms & coordinated inauthenticity** – free sign-up + anonymity =
   uncontrollable inauthentic behaviour.

## Solution

Vera's "Verify & Shred" entry protocol:

- **Age-gate**: Every user must pass a government-ID age check (Persona) before
  accessing the platform.  Under-18 traffic is blocked at the architecture layer,
  not the policy layer.
- **Zero-data model**: ID documents are processed exclusively by Persona.  Vera
  stores only a boolean verification flag.  A data breach exposes nothing.
- **Pay-to-join**: A nominal $3.99 CAD one-time fee and $0.99/month subscription
  removes the economic incentive for bot operators (cost per bot × scale =
  non-viable) while funding the platform without advertising.

## Go-to-Market

1. **Canadian launch** – align with Bill C-63 enforcement timeline; market
   Vera as the compliant alternative for content creators leaving US platforms.
2. **Creator partnerships** – onboard 50 verified Canadian creators pre-launch
   to seed authentic content.
3. **Press strategy** – position as the privacy-respecting, regulation-forward
   platform in national media.
4. **Organic growth** – verified-network effects (every user is real) create a
   high-trust environment that is self-reinforcing.

## Acquisition Value Proposition

Vera's primary value to an acquirer is **regulatory insurance**:

- A platform with Vera's architecture is **pre-compliant** with Canada's Online
  Harms Act and analogous global legislation.
- The "Verify & Shred" patent application protects the zero-PII verification
  methodology.
- The existing user base is 100% verified adult humans – the highest-quality
  social graph available.
